Aura — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

Controller: Jøran Igland, Norway · Contact: utkingbank@gmail.com

The short version

Aura runs entirely on your device. Your photos, videos, file names, hashes, and folders never leave your phone. We don't have accounts, we don't track you, and we can't see your files.

What the app does with your data

  • All scanning, hashing, similarity detection, EXIF reading, and comparisons happen locally on your device. No file content, file names, paths, thumbnails, or metadata are transmitted anywhere.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising IDs.
  • No account is required or created.
  • The app makes no network calls to us. App updates are delivered through the Google Play Store.
  • Pro is unlocked on-device based on your Google Play purchase (see below).

Purchases (Aura Pro)

Aura Pro is a one-time purchase. On Android, the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment — we never see or store your card details. The app receives only a confirmation from Google Play that a valid purchase exists for your Google account, which it uses to unlock Pro features. We do not collect your name, email, or payment information through the app.

Google's handling of your payment is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Retention

The app stores your scan cache and settings only on your device; you can clear them at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling. We do not hold a copy of any of it. Purchase/entitlement records are maintained by Google Play, not by us.

Your rights (GDPR / EEA)

Because the app collects no personal data on our servers, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, or delete. For any data Google Play holds about your purchase, use Google's own privacy controls. You may contact us at utkingbank@gmail.com with any question, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Norway, Datatilsynet).

Children

Aura is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We'll update this page and the "Last updated" date if this policy changes.
