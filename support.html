Contact

Need help with Aura? Email utkingbank@gmail.com. Please mention whether you use Android or iOS and include any details that may help us understand the issue.

Frequently asked questions

Does Aura delete photos automatically?

No. Photos are only deleted after user confirmation. On iOS, deleted photos remain in Recently Deleted for 30 days. On Android, deleted photos are moved to the system trash where supported.

What can I use for free?

Exact-duplicate cleanup is free. Aura Pro unlocks additional features and is a one-time purchase on both Android and iOS.

Where can I read Aura's privacy policy?

See the Aura Privacy Policy. Scanning and hashing stay fully on-device on both Android and iOS.
